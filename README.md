## 📖 About GLX Media Android TV

### Your media library on the TV

GLX Media Android TV brings your films, series, music and audio books from your GLX Media Server to the TV. With covers, descriptions and resume playback, fully usable with the d-pad of your TV remote.

### The GLX Media family

The main program is GLX Media Server. It runs at your home and manages libraries, covers, users and resume playback. With a GLX Media Server you can use GLX Media Windows for the PC, GLX Media Android TV for the TV, GLX Media Smartphone for your phone and GLX Remote as a remote control, all free of charge. On iPhone and iPad you install GLX Media as a web app on the home screen via Safari. Whatever you change on one device shows up on all others right away.

### Everything stays in sync

Whatever you change on the TV, for example covers, favourites or watched episodes, is saved on the server and shows up right away on the PC, in the browser and on your phone. Start on the PC, continue on the TV.

### GLX Player with VLC

Films play in the GLX Player with the VLC engine, so MKV with AC3 or DTS sound works too. Audio track and subtitles are chosen right in the player.

### New player controls

The player controls are slim and show clear icons only: previous title, rewind, play or pause, fast forward and next title. Below you see position, remaining time and total running time. The title only appears when you pause. Repeat and shuffle are switched with a single button.

### D-pad like Kodi

While a film is playing, left and right rewind and fast forward, up and down jump 10 minutes forward or back, without any menu.

### Resume and markers

In the player you set a marker at your favourite spot. On the next start GLX Media asks whether you want to resume, start from the marker or play from the beginning.

### Music and audio books

The music and audio book player opens full size and keeps playing while you browse the library.

### GLX Remote: your phone as a remote control

GLX Remote turns your phone into a remote control: d-pad, touchpad, keyboard, playback keys and jumps straight to your libraries. For Android there is the GLX Remote app, which you download right in the TV app under Settings › Remote control. On the iPhone you open the address shown in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen". The remote layout can only be edited with a double tap, so nothing moves by accident.

### Easy to connect

The app finds your server in your home network by itself. You sign in comfortably with your phone via QR code. Text is entered with the GLX keyboard or from your phone via GLX Remote.

### Fits every TV

Size and screen margin are set right in the app, so nothing is cut off at the edge. The 12 GLX designs and 10 icon sets you chose in GLX Media apply here too.

### Built-in updates

The app updates itself. New versions only come from glx-tools.de and are checked with a checksum.

### Free of charge

GLX Media Android TV is free. Licence and trial only apply to GLX Media Server. For TVs without a server there is the standalone app GLX Media TV One.
